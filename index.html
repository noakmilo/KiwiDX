Station Logbook

Precision Shortwave & Ham Logging

Eliminate manual record-keeping errors. Right-clicking the dial instantly prefills active receiver parameters, carrier frequency, modulation, UTC timestamp, and antenna configuration.

[Shortwave Log Entry] Station: BBC World Service Freq: 12095.000 kHz AM | Rx: Enschede NL SIMPO: S4 I4 M4 P3 O4 (Signal: Good) Antenna: Mini-Whip 20m High AGL

Supports SIMPO evaluations (Signal, Interference, Noise, Propagation, Overall) and provides single-report UTF-8 text file exports for QSL verification.
